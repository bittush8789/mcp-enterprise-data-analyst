# Enterprise Commercial Refund Policy

Document Title: Global Enterprise Software & Services Refund Policy
Policy Type: Commercial Terms & Customer Financial Operations
Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Version: 4.2
Applicable Customer Segment: Enterprise, Mid-Market, SMB

## 1. Overview and Scope
This policy governs refund eligibility, service credit adjustments, and termination rights for all commercial customers utilizing enterprise software licenses, cloud platform subscriptions, and professional consulting services.

## 2. Standard Refund Terms
- 30-Day Evaluation Window: Enterprise and Mid-Market customers entering a new multi-year Master Services Agreement (MSA) are entitled to a full refund within thirty (30) calendar days of the initial provisioning date if the software platform fails to meet documented functional specifications.
- SMB Purchases: Small and Medium Business (SMB) customers purchasing via direct credit terms are eligible for a refund within fourteen (14) calendar days of purchase, subject to non-usage verification.
- Pro-Rata Cloud Credits: After the standard evaluation window, refunds are not issued in cash or bank transfer; eligible account balance adjustments are granted as prorated service credits applicable toward future billing cycles.

## 3. SLA Service Credit Adjustments
If system uptime drops below our contractual Service Level Agreement (SLA) of 99.99%:
- System Uptime 99.00% - 99.89%: 10% monthly subscription credit.
- System Uptime 95.00% - 98.99%: 25% monthly subscription credit.
- System Uptime below 95.00%: 50% monthly subscription credit or right to terminate without penalty.

## 4. Exceptions and Non-Refundable Items
The following items are strictly non-refundable:
- Professional services, customized deployment, data migration, and onboarding hours once work has commenced.
- Consumed cloud compute, storage egress, and third-party API execution units.
- Accounts terminated due to violation of Acceptable Use Policies or security breaches caused by customer negligence.

## 5. Refund Submission and Approval Procedure
1. Submission: The customer must submit a formal refund request via the Customer Portal attaching invoice numbers and technical logs.
2. Review: Customer Success Operations validates invoice payment status within two (2) business days.
3. Approval Hierarchy:
   - Up to $10,000: Approved by Customer Success Manager (CSM).
   - $10,001 - $50,000: Approved by Director of Commercial Operations.
   - Above $50,000: Requires authorization from the Chief Financial Officer (CFO).
4. Credit Issuance: Approved refunds/credits are processed within five (5) business days and appear on the subsequent monthly statement.
